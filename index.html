📚 Quick Start Guides

      
      

        
        

          

            🚀 How to use Live Load Tracking?
          

          

            Go to the Live Location tab, enter your Load ID, pickup/dropoff cities, and driver's WhatsApp number. Click Finalize Deal & Activate Tracking to instantly dispatch confirmation details via WhatsApp.
          

        


        

          

            🛡️ How to verify Brokers or Truckers?
          

          

            Open the Account Status directory. Type any company name, MC number, or DOT number in the search bar to instantly check their verification status, insurance standing, and safety records.
          

        


        

          

            💰 How does the Profit Calculator work?
          

          

            Input your total trip miles, offered broker rate, and diesel price. The system automatically computes your net profit, fuel expenses, and Rate Per Mile (RPM) instantly.
          

        


      

    


  



      
      


  

    
    
    

      

        

          ❓ Frequently Asked Questions
        

        
Everything you need to know about LoadAgent-AI features and operations

      

      
        ● Knowledge Base
      
    


    
    

      
      
      

        

          Q. What is LoadAgent-AI and who is it built for?
        

        

          LoadAgent-AI is a mobile-first, zero-friction dispatching suite built specifically for independent freight dispatchers, freight brokers, and owner-operator truckers to manage loads, calculate profits, track drivers live, and verify credentials instantly[cite: 1].
        

      


      
      

        

          Q. Do I need an API key or desktop software to run it?
        

        

          Not at all! LoadAgent-AI is completely web-based and optimized for mobile phones[cite: 1]. It requires zero complex API setup or installations, allowing you to run your entire dispatch workflow directly from your browser.
        

      


      
      

        

          Q. How does live truck tracking work without complex hardware?
        

        

          Dispatchers can finalize a load and instantly send structured check-in links to drivers via WhatsApp or SMS. Drivers can click and share their live GPS or drop manual city/highway check-ins that update the interactive map in real time.
        

      


      
      

        

          Q. How does the Trust & Verification Directory work?
        

        

          The directory allows dispatchers, brokers, and truckers to search company names, MC numbers, or DOT numbers to check compliance status and avoid fraudulent entities before booking loads[cite: 1].
        

      


      
      

        

          Q. Is my data safe while testing on the platform?
        

        

          Yes. All active states, profiles, and configurations are securely cached in your browser's local storage for lightning-fast responsiveness, with options to export or reset your data anytime from settings.
